Fasih-TTS-V1

فصيح

Modern Standard Arabic text to speech — the voice the companion answers in. Fine-tuned from XTTS v2 on 1,297 fully-diacritized clips, with its own Arabic front-end that adds tashkīl, expands numbers and chunks long text before a word is spoken.

عربيّة فصيحة، بنطقٍ صحيح.
01

What it does

ما يفعله

Five capabilities, each in the words of the model card and each with one Arabic line for the posters. Every one of them is a measured claim; section 02 shows the measurement.

011.3%
Human-level intelligibility

Human-level intelligibility — 1.3% CER (ASR-measured), matching the 1.8% error floor of the original human recordings. The synthetic voice is as understandable as the actor.

وضوح بمستوى الإنسان.
020 / 24
Broadcast consistency

Broadcast consistency — the same sentence synthesized 4× gave identical CER, and across 24 stress generations there were zero autoregressive failures (no loops, skips, or early cut-offs) — the classic XTTS weakness, eliminated.

ثابت في كلّ مرّة.
03إعراب
True Fusha, correct iʿrāb

True Fusha, correct iʿrāb — trained on fully-diacritized text and paired with a built-in CATT diacritizer, so even bare, undiacritized Arabic is pronounced correctly, case-endings and all.

فصحى صحيحة الإعراب.
041 call
Production-ready text handling

Production-ready text handling — auto-expands numbers to words, applies a sacred-term pronunciation lexicon, and chunks long passages — raw assistant text becomes speech-ready in a single call.

نصّ خام، كلام جاهز.
05675 ms
Real-time and streaming

Real-time and streaming — RTF ≈ 0.60, streaming first-audio ≈ 675 ms, clean 24 kHz output — suitable for live conversational agents.

يتكلّم في الوقت نفسه.

How to read it
The human recordings themselves score 1.8%, because Whisper misreads clean Arabic too. That line is the floor: a bar to its left is as understandable as the actor. Three of four synthetic sets sit at or under it.
The 8.2% is shown, not hidden
The hard-stress set's worst clip is drawn at full length. A chart that trims its worst case is the visual version of an answer with no source.
Clip counts travel with the number
2 to 8 clips per set. Wherever a CER appears on a banner, the set and its size are on the card beneath it.

05

Saying it right

كيف نقولها
Every number names its test
1.3% is the varied-MSA set, Whisper judge, 8 clips. Say "1.3% CER on varied MSA" on a card; on a banner the card beneath carries the rest.
Intelligibility is not naturalness
Fasih leads on WER and is third on UTMOS. Never imply it is the most natural voice; say it is the most correctly pronounced, which is the measured claim.
Demo with prose
Every audio sample is a sentence of ordinary Arabic — a greeting, a ruling's explanation, a date. Never an āyah, not even read plainly. Recitation belongs to the named reciters.
